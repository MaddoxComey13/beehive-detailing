FAQ

Can you get pet hair out?

Yes — pet hair removal is available as an add-on at checkout, and it's included at the Gold and Diamond tiers. Heavy coats are no problem.

Do you remove smoke or pet odors?

We offer dedicated odor removal, including a cigarette-smoke treatment. It's a quick add-on when you book.

Will you get old stains out?

Most of them. Stain removal and carpet shampoo lift the majority of set-in spots — some permanent dye or bleach damage can't be reversed, but we'll get it as close as possible.
