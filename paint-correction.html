FAQ

Good to know.

How much does paint correction cost?

It depends on your vehicle's size, paint condition, and how many stages of correction it needs. Send us a few photos and we'll give you a clear, no-pressure quote.

What's the difference between correction and a polish?

Correction removes defects like swirls and scratches from the clear coat. A polish enhances gloss and cleans up very light imperfections but isn't full defect removal. We'll recommend the right one for your paint.

Should I get ceramic coating after?

It's the ideal combo — correct the paint to perfect, then ceramic coat it so that flawless finish is protected for years.
