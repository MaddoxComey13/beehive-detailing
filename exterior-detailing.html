FAQ

Good to know.

Is this a hand wash or a machine wash?

Always by hand. We never run your car through automatic brushes — that's what causes swirl marks and scratches. Every wash is done by hand with clean media.

How long does the wax last?

A hand wax typically protects and beads for several weeks to a couple of months, depending on weather and how often you drive. For multi-year protection, ask about ceramic coating.

Can you get water spots and hard grime off?

Yes. Decontamination and a proper multi-step wash lift bonded grime, bugs, and tar that a normal wash leaves behind — leaving the paint smooth and glossy.
