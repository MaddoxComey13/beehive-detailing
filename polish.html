FAQ

Good to know.

Polish or paint correction — what do I need?

A polish enhances gloss and softens very light imperfections in one step. Paint correction actually removes swirls and scratches over multiple stages. If your paint has real defects, correction is the answer; if it's healthy and you just want more shine, polish is perfect.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your vehicle's size and paint condition. Send us a quick note and we'll give you a clear quote.

Can I add ceramic after?

Absolutely — polishing first then a ceramic coating gives a brilliant, long-lasting finish.
