Why ceramic coating

FAQ

Good to know.

How much does ceramic coating cost?

It varies by vehicle size, the condition of the paint, and how much correction it needs first. Send us a quick note about your car and we'll get you a clear, no-pressure quote.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

Depending on the product and how the car is cared for, a professional coating typically lasts multiple years. We'll walk you through the options when we quote it.

Is it better than wax?

For longevity and protection, yes — a coating lasts years where wax lasts weeks. Wax is still great for a quick shine; ceramic is the long-term investment.

Do you do it mobile?

We handle prep and coating with mobile service where conditions allow. We'll confirm the best setup for your car when you request a quote.
